About Our Foundation




Our Story

Edupower Youth Foundation is a youth-led, nonprofit organization founded on 16th July 2021, in South Sudan, and legally registered with the Relief and Rehabilitation Commission (RRC) under certificate number 3748.




Our Purpose

We exist to empower youth through transformative education, leadership, development and innovation programs that tackle inequality in access to quality learning and skills.




Who We Serve

We serve underprivileged students and communities, particularly girls and youth in marginalized areas, by equipping them with the tools, knowledge and platforms to thrive in school, life and society.

Vision & Mission






Our Vision

A South Sudan where every young person has equitable access to learning, mentorship and tools to thrive in a knowledge-driven world.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap in access, skills and opportunities for underprivileged youth by providing educational resources, leadership development and higher education support.






Our Strategic Focus






Strategic Objectives


  • Promote practical and inclusive education across South Sudan.

  • Equip youth with 21st-century skills in leadership, entrepreneurship, and digital literacy.

  • Foster community-driven solutions through education and innovation.

  • Build sustainable learning environments in underserved areas.



Strategic Goals by 2030


  • Build 10 youth learning hubs across 10 states

  • Established & support 25 schools with libraries in underserved communities

  • Train 10,000 youth in entrepreneurial leadership and peacebuilding by 2035

  • Train 250 global scholarships and opportunitiesq
